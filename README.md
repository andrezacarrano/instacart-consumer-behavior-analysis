
# 🛒 Instacart Consumer Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores consumer purchasing behavior on Instacart, an online grocery delivery platform.

The main objective is to identify purchasing patterns, including order timing, busiest days of the week, popular products, reorder frequency, and other behavioral trends.

From a business perspective, understanding these patterns can help improve operational planning, optimize marketing strategies, and enhance the overall customer experience.

## 🎯 Business Questions

- Which days of the week have the highest order volume?
- At what times of the day do customers place the most orders?
- Which departments and products are the most popular?
- How many products do customers typically purchase per order?
- Which products are most frequently reordered?
- How much time typically passes between consecutive orders?

## 📊 Key Findings

The exploratory data analysis revealed several insights into consumer purchasing behavior:

- **Busiest day:** Sunday (represented as `0` in the dataset) recorded the highest number of orders.
- **Peak ordering hours:** Most orders were placed between 10 AM and 11 AM.
- **Most popular department:** Produce (fresh fruits and vegetables) had the highest number of products ordered.
- **Top products:** The 20 most frequently ordered products included bananas, milk, eggs, and various fruits.
- **Average basket size:** Customers purchased approximately 10 items per order.
- **Most reordered products:** Bananas and milk were among the products with the highest reorder frequency.
- **Average reorder interval:** Customers placed orders approximately every 11 days.

Data preprocessing also involved identifying and removing duplicate records and handling missing values to improve data quality and support the analysis.
